📖 Fairness Cheat-Sheet

Statistical Parity

Are selection rates equal across groups? E.g., Do men and women get hired at the same rate?

💡 Mnemonic: "Parity = Proportions"

Equal Opportunity

Are qualified candidates from all groups selected equally? E.g., Do qualified women get hired at the same rate as qualified men?

💡 Mnemonic: "Opportunity = Outcomes for the Qualified"

Disparate Impact

Often measured by the 80% rule. The selection rate of the unprivileged group should be at least 80% of the privileged group.

💡 Mnemonic: "Disparate = Division (Ratio)"
